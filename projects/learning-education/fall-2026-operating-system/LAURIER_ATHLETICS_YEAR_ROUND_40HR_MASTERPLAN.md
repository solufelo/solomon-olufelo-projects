## 🛠️ The 4-Pillar Work Spawning Engine (How to Justify 40 Hrs/Wk)

Hailey Tripodi loves proactive producers who take initiative without needing micromanagement. Games only take ~8 hours on Saturdays; the other 32 hours are spawned across these four pillars:

### Pillar 1: Game-Day Operations & Control Room (8–10 Hours / Week)
* Control room tech call, Ross Video / Daktronics LED cue sheet calibration.
* Live camera operation, instant replay clipping, and real-time animation triggering.
* Cable strike, gear breakdown, and media card offloading.

### Pillar 2: 3D Motion Graphics & Blender Asset Pipelines (12–15 Hours / Week)
* **Football**: Re-skinning 3D helmet assets with opponent decals (e.g., Western Mustang head, Guelph Gryphon, McMaster Marauder).
* **Basketball**: Modeling the Athletic Complex (AC) hardwood court in Blender with glowing PBR floor reflections for player starting-five intros.
* **Hockey**: Creating 3D puck shatter, ice spray, and siren light assets for Sun Life Financial Arena.
* **Crowd Prompt Packs**: Designing 5-second 60fps render loops: *MAKE NOISE*, *3RD DOWN*, *TOUCHDOWN SLAM*, *AIR BALL*, *POWER PLAY*, *DE-FENSE*.

### Pillar 3: Social Media & Vertical Video Content (8–10 Hours / Week)
* Cutting 1080x1920 9:16 high-energy hype reels for `@WLUAthletics` Instagram & TikTok.
* Post-game soundbites paired with motion typography and official brand fonts (`ariblk.ttf`, Radwave).
* "Plays of the Week" top-5 countdown edits with sound design.

### Pillar 4: Sponsor Integration & Digital Asset Archiving (5–8 Hours / Week)
* Animating commercial sponsor loops (Domino's, RBC, Booster Juice, Wilfrid Laurier Alumni).
* Managing footage backups across internal NVMe and external portable drives.
* Video logging, tagging player jersey numbers for TSN/OUA broadcast requests.
