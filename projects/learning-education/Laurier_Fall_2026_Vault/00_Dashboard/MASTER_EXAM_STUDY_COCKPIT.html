FOUNDATIONAL MENTAL MODEL

The Research Trinity & Inductive Qualitative Logic

1. Ontology
What is real?

Does an objective physical reality exist independently (Discovery), or is reality socially constructed and multiple (Interpretation)?

2. Epistemology
How do we know it?

Can a researcher be a detached, objective observer ("God's eye view"), or are the knower and the known completely inseparable?

3. Methodology
How do we study it?

Deductive quantitative testing of rigid hypotheses vs. inductive qualitative immersion (interviews, ethnography, observation).

Dr. Svenson's Core Principle: Qualitative research uses inductive logic—you don't walk into the field with pre-cooked hypotheses; you observe human interactions and let grounded theory emerge!

Grosfoguel's 4 Epistemicides & The Cartesian Link

1. 1492 — Al-Andalus (Spain):

Expulsion of Moors & Jews; burning of massive Arabic/Hebrew scientific, medical, and philosophical libraries to enforce Christian epistemic monopoly.

2. 1492–1600s — Abya Yala (Americas):

Indigenous genocide paired with systematic burning of sacred Mayan, Aztec, and Andean codices to eradicate non-Western legal and ecological science.

3. 1500s–1800s — Transatlantic Slave Trade:

Kidnapping millions of Africans, commodifying them into chattel, and violently stripping away ancestral cosmologies, metallurgical arts, and languages.

4. 1500s–1600s — European Witch Hunts:

Burning hundreds of thousands of female midwives and plant-healers to destroy autonomous feminine ecological wisdom in favor of patriarchal medical science.

The Cartesian Link: René Descartes' Ego cogito ("I think, therefore I am") claimed universal detached truth. But Grosfoguel reveals it was preceded by 150 years of Ego conquiro ("I conquer, therefore I am")—imperial militarism disguised as neutral rationality.

MCCLOUD CHAPTER 3 CHEAT SHEET

The 6 Panel-to-Panel Transitions

1. Moment-to-Moment:

Fraction of a second (eyes open $\rightarrow$ blink). Minimal closure.

2. Action-to-Action:

Single subject in progression (pitcher winds up $\rightarrow$ hits ball). Western standard.

3. Subject-to-Subject:

Same scene, shifts focus (runner crosses line $\rightarrow$ coach's stopwatch).

4. Scene-to-Scene:

Major time/space jump ("10 years later" or NY $\rightarrow$ Paris). Deductive reasoning.

5. Aspect-to-Aspect:

Time stops; camera wanders over mood/place (snow, bell, lantern). Manga style.

6. Non-Sequitur:

Zero logical link (apple $\rightarrow$ car). Pure absurdity/surrealism.
