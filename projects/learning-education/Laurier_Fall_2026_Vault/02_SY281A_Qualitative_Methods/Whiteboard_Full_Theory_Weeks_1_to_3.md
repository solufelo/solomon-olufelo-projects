## 1.1 The Research Trinity
* **Ontology:** What is the nature of reality? (Objective singular truth vs. multiple constructed truths).
* **Epistemology:** How do we know what we know? (Relationship between knower and known).
* **Methodology:** How do we systematically study it? (Qualitative immersion vs. quantitative measurement).

---

## 1.2 Grosfoguel's 4 Epistemicides (Long 16th Century)
* Epistemicide = Systematic destruction and invalidation of subaltern knowledge systems.
* 1492: Conquest of Al-Andalus (Muslims & Jews expelled, libraries burned).
* 1492–1600s: Conquest of Abya Yala / Americas (Indigenous genocide, codices burned).
* 1500s–1800s: Transatlantic slave trade (Africans commodified, languages and cosmologies stripped).
* 1500s–1600s: European witch hunts (midwives and healers burned to establish capitalist patriarchal science).
* **Descartes Connection:** *Ego conquiro* (I conquer) historically preceded *Ego cogito* (I think).

## 1.4 Peirce's 4 Ways of Knowing (T-A-P-S)
* **T = Tenacity:** Habit and tradition ("always been this way").
* **A = Authority:** Believed because an expert or institution decrees it.
* **P = A Priori:** Believed because it seems intuitively logical or reasonable.
* **S = Science:** Systematic empirical observation, peer testing, qualitative analysis.

---

## 1.5 Berger & Luckmann — Social Construction (D-U-R-C)
* **D = Definition:** Reality is created and maintained via social interaction.
* **U = Ubiquitous:** Omnipresent throughout everyday routines, language, and customs.
* **R = Real in Consequences:** Thomas Theorem ("If men define situations as real, they are real in their consequences").
* **C = Co-constructed:** Built through language, habits, institutionalization, and socialization.

---

## 1.6 Merrigan's 3 Research Paradigms
* **Interpretation:** Multiple realities, subjective meaning, ethnography (Dr. Svenson).
* **Discovery:** Singular objective reality, detached observer, quantitative stats.
* **Criticism:** Power hierarchies, ideology, exposing oppression, social change.
* **Schutz's Lifeworld:** The inter-subjective common-sense world taken for granted.
